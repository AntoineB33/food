Give me a list of categories that together contain all the recipes (and food that can be eaten without being in a recipe in a good meal) that are healthy and vegan in the category Suspensions, Soups & Stews. An item should not be possibly found in several categories.





To create a mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive list for healthy, vegan items within the overarching "Suspensions, Soups & Stews" category, the most effective method is to classify them by their **physical texture, viscosity, and solid-to-liquid ratio**. 

By using a physical state "decision tree," an item cannot physically exist in two categories at once (e.g., a dish cannot be entirely pureed and also full of distinct chunks). 

Here are the four categories that encompass all healthy, vegan items in this space without overlapping:

### 1. Gel-Based Suspensions (Semi-Solid Matrix)
This category includes foods where the liquid has been thickened into a gel or semi-solid state using natural vegan hydrocolloids (like chia, flax, starch, or agar), holding particles in a permanent suspension. 
* **Defining characteristic:** The base is no longer a free-flowing liquid, and ingredients are locked in a semi-solid matrix.
* **Healthy & Vegan Examples:** Chia seed puddings (made with plant milk), overnight oats soaked in soy milk, tapioca pearl puddings, and savory vegetable aspic/agar gels.

### 2. Homogenized Purees & Emulsions (Zero Distinct Solids)
This category includes all liquids, soups, and suspensions that have been fully blended, pureed, or emulsified. They flow freely (unlike gels) but contain no chewable chunks or distinct pieces.
* **Defining characteristic:** A uniformly smooth texture from start to finish. If you must chew it, it belongs in another category.
* **Healthy & Vegan Examples:** Blended butternut squash soup, smooth cashew-based vegan "cream" of tomato soup, green smoothies, protein shakes, and pureed gazpacho. 

### 3. Broth-Based Soups & Suspensions (Thin Liquid + Distinct Solids)
This category contains dishes where distinct, chewable solid ingredients are suspended in or surrounded by a low-viscosity, watery liquid base. 
* **Defining characteristic:** The liquid runs like water or clear broth, and the solid-to-liquid ratio is usually lower (meaning there is plenty of free liquid).
* **Healthy & Vegan Examples:** Clear vegetable broths with diced root vegetables, miso soup with wakame and silken tofu, vegan pho, minestrone (with a thin tomato broth), and infused waters with suspended basil/falooda seeds.

### 4. High-Viscosity Stews & Chilis (Thick Liquid + Distinct Solids)
This category captures dishes featuring distinct, chewable solids suspended in a highly viscous, thick liquid, sauce, or gravy. 
* **Defining characteristic:** The liquid coats the back of a spoon and the solid-to-liquid ratio is very high, meaning the solids are stacked on top of one another rather than floating freely.
* **Healthy & Vegan Examples:** Hearty three-bean chili, thick lentil stews, chunky chickpea and spinach curries, and West African peanut stew.

**Why this works:** If you have a healthy vegan dish in mind, simply ask: Is it gelled? (If yes -> **Category 1**). If no, does it have chunks? (If no -> **Category 2**). If yes, is the liquid thin and watery or thick and heavy? (Thin -> **Category 3**, Thick -> **Category 4**).
