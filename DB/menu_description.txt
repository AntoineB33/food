### Breakfast

* **Pea protein isolate**: 40g (~1 heaping scoop)
* **Bananas, raw**: 65g (~1/2 medium banana)
* **Seeds, hemp seed, hulled**: 28g (~3 tablespoons)
* **Beverages, water, tap, municipal**: 500g (~2 cups)
* **Preparation & Tips**: Blend these together into a protein shake. The dietary fat and protein from the hemp seeds and pea isolate will slow the absorption of the simple carbohydrates in the banana, perfectly managing your glycemic load to prevent an insulin spike.

### Mid-Morning (Between Meals)

* **Beverages, tea, black, brewed, prepared with tap water**: 120g (~1/2 cup)
* **Beverages, water, tap, municipal**: 460g (~2 cups)
* **Preparation & Tips**: Drink your black tea completely apart from any meals. This ensures that the tannins in the tea do not block the absorption of plant-based iron from your solid foods.

### Lunch

* **Couscous, dry**: 155g (~1 cup dry)
* **Pea protein isolate**: 40g (~1 heaping scoop)
* **Nuts, pistachio nuts, raw**: 50g (~1/3 cup)
* **Salt, table**: 2g (~1/3 teaspoon)
* **Beverages, water, tap, municipal**: 1000g (~4 cups, partially used to cook the couscous)
* **Preparation & Tips**: Cook the couscous in boiling water and salt, then mix in the savory pea protein and top with raw pistachios. This provides complex carbohydrates paired with protein and fat. You are also varying your protein sources (pea, wheat from couscous, and nuts) without relying on soy.

### Dinner

* **Rice, white, long-grain, regular, raw, unenriched**: 135g (~3/4 cup dry)
* **Tofu, raw, firm, prepared with calcium sulfate**: 160g (~1/2 block)
* **Nuts, cashew nuts, raw**: 50g (~1/3 cup)
* **Salt, table**: 2g (~1/3 teaspoon)
* **Vegan multivitamin**: 1 dose
* **Algae oil supplement, pill**: 1 dose
* **Choline supplement, pill**: 2 doses
* **Beverages, water, tap, municipal**: 1000g (~4 cups, partially used to cook the rice)
* **Preparation & Tips**: Cook the rice in water and salt. Pan-fry or bake the tofu and top it with crushed cashews. Take your multivitamin, algae oil, and choline with this meal: the healthy fats in the cashews and oil will ensure the fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, and K) absorb properly. Additionally, by placing your calcium-set tofu at dinner, you successfully separate your heavy calcium intake from the iron sources you ate earlier in the day. Your soy intake is also kept strictly to this single meal (160g), providing far less than half of your daily protein total.
