### Corrected Menu

**Breakfast (Iron, Fat-Soluble Vitamin & Vit C Focus)**

* **Oatmeal**: Cook 65g of "Cereals, oats, regular and quick, not fortified, dry" (~3/4 cup) using 200g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
* **Toppings**: Stir in 25g of "Peanut butter, smooth style, without salt" (~1.5 tbsp), 30g of "Seeds, sunflower seed kernels, dry roasted, without salt" (~3 tbsp), and 100g of "Strawberries, frozen, unsweetened". The strawberries provide the Vitamin C needed to absorb the oats' iron, plus anthocyanins for your color spectrum.
* **Supplements**: Take 1 dose of "Vegan multivitamin", 1 dose of "Algae oil supplement, pill", and 1 dose of "Choline supplement, pill".
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".

**Mid-Morning Snack (Calcium & Soy Protein Focus)**

* **Protein Shake**: Blend 515g of "Soymilk, original and vanilla, with added calcium, vitamins A and D" (~2.1 cups) with 50g of "Soy protein isolate" (~1.5 scoops).
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".

**Lunch (Protein Diversity & Complex Carbs)**

* **Rice**: Cook 135g of "Rice, brown, long-grain, raw" (~3/4 cup dry). It will absorb roughly 300g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" during cooking. Brown rice provides the complex carbs needed for steady blood sugar.
* **Seitan**: Make a dough using 50g of "Vital wheat gluten", 14g of "Nutritional yeast" (~2.5 tbsp), 2g of "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp), and 50g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal". **Boil or steam the dough for 30 minutes** so it becomes safely digestible. Once cooked, pan-fry it using 15g of "Oil, canola" (~1 tbsp).
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".

**Dinner (Complex Carbs, Fats & Lycopene)**

* **Pasta**: Boil 80g of "Pasta, dry, enriched". It will absorb approximately 150g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" as it cooks.
* **Sauce/Seasoning**: Toss the cooked pasta with 100g of "Tomato products, canned, puree, without salt added" (providing lycopene), the remaining 20g of "Oil, canola" (~1.5 tbsp), 15g of "Nutritional yeast" (~3 tbsp), and the remaining 2g of "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp).
* **Hydration**: Drink your final 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
