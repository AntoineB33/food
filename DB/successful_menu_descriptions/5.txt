**Breakfast (Iron & Fat-Soluble Vitamin Focus)**

* **Oatmeal**: Cook 65g of "Cereals, oats, regular and quick, not fortified, dry" (~3/4 cup) using 200g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
* **Toppings**: Stir in 25g of "Peanut butter, smooth style, without salt" (~1.5 tbsp) and 30g of "Seeds, sunflower seed kernels, dry roasted, without salt" (~3 tbsp). Pairing these complex carbs with the fiber, fat, and protein of the seeds and peanut butter perfectly manages your glycemic load.
* **Supplements**: Take 1 dose of "Vegan multivitamin", 1 dose of "Algae oil supplement, pill", and 1 dose of "Choline supplement, pill". Because you are consuming fat from the peanut butter and sunflower seeds, your intestinal wall will efficiently absorb the fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, and K) in your multivitamin.
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
*(Note: No tea is included here to prevent tannins from blocking the plant-based iron in the oats. No calcium sources are included here either.)*

**Mid-Morning Snack (Calcium & Soy Protein Focus)**

* **Protein Shake**: Blend 515g of "Soymilk, original and vanilla, with added calcium, vitamins A and D" (~2.1 cups) with 50g of "Soy protein isolate" (~1.5 scoops).
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
*(Note: Consuming your calcium-fortified soymilk as a standalone snack completely separates it from your iron-rich meals, preventing calcium from blocking iron absorption. This also restricts your soy intake to exactly 2 foods that provide around 60g of protein—less than half of your day's total protein—ensuring protein diversity.)*

**Lunch (Protein Diversity & Complex Carbs)**

* **Rice**: Cook 135g of "Rice, white, long-grain, regular, raw, unenriched" (~3/4 cup dry). It will absorb roughly 300g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" during cooking.
* **Seitan**: Make a dough using 50g of "Vital wheat gluten", 14g of "Nutritional yeast" (~2.5 tbsp), 2g of "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp), and 50g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal". Pan-fry the seitan using 15g of "Oil, canola" (~1 tbsp).
* **Hydration**: Drink 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
*(Note: Wheat gluten and nutritional yeast introduce new protein families, diversifying your intake away from soy while providing a low-glycemic meal.)*

**Dinner (Complex Carbs & Fats)**

* **Pasta**: Boil 80g of "Pasta, dry, enriched" (a standard single portion). It will absorb approximately 150g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" as it cooks.
* **Sauce/Seasoning**: Toss the cooked pasta with the remaining 20g of "Oil, canola" (~1.5 tbsp), 15g of "Nutritional yeast" (~3 tbsp), and the remaining 2g of "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp).
* **Hydration**: Drink your final 500g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
