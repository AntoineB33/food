### Corrected Menu

**Breakfast (Iron, Fat-Soluble Vitamin & Vit C Focus)**

* **Oatmeal**: Cook 35g of "Cereals, oats, regular and quick, not fortified, dry" (~1/3 cup) using 100g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
* **Toppings**: Stir in 25g of "Peanut butter, smooth style, without salt" (~1.5 tbsp), 28g of "Seeds, sunflower seed kernels, dry roasted, without salt" (~3 tbsp), and 40g of "Strawberries, frozen, unsweetened" (~1/4 cup) for Vitamin C.
* **Supplements**: Take 1 dose of "Vegan multivitamin", 1 dose of "Algae oil supplement, pill", and 1 dose of "Choline supplement, pill".
* **Hydration**: Drink 520g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".

**Mid-Morning Snack (Protein & Iron Absorption Focus)**

* **Protein Shake**: Blend 59g of "Soy protein isolate" (~2 scoops) with 520g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" and the remaining 40g of "Strawberries, frozen, unsweetened" (~1/4 cup). The strawberries provide the Vitamin C necessary to overcome the soy's phytic acid, allowing you to absorb its high iron content.

**Lunch (Protein Diversity & Complex Carbs)**

* **Rice**: Cook 145g of "Rice, brown, long-grain, raw" (~3/4 cup dry). To save time, buy a 10-minute quick-cook variety (available at your local Auchan or Carrefour). It will absorb roughly 320g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" during cooking.
* **Seitan**: Use your weekend batch-cooked dough (made from 50g of "Vital wheat gluten", 7g of "Nutritional yeast", 2g of iodized "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp), and 50g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal"). Pan-fry this portion in a non-stick pan *without* oil to save time and prevent burning fats.
* **Healthy Fats**: Once on the plate, pour 15g of "Oil, canola" (~1 tbsp) *raw* over your rice and seitan to preserve its essential Omega-3s.
* **Hydration**: Drink 520g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".

**Afternoon Snack (Calcium Focus)**

* **Calcium Boost**: Drink 605g of "Soymilk, original and vanilla, with added calcium, vitamins A and D" (~2.5 cups). Consuming this separately ensures calcium does not interfere with your plant-based iron.

**Dinner (Complex Carbs, Fats & Lycopene)**

* **Pasta**: Boil 80g of "Pasta, dry, enriched". It will absorb approximately 150g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal" as it cooks.
* **Sauce/Seasoning**: Toss the cooked pasta with 100g of "Tomato products, canned, puree, without salt added", the remaining 20g of "Oil, canola" (~1.5 tbsp), the remaining 8g of "Nutritional yeast" (~1.5 tbsp), and the remaining 2g of iodized "Salt, table" (~1/3 tsp).
* **Hydration**: Drink your final 520g of "Beverages, water, tap, municipal".
